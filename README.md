# Ergon

Showroom pages for Taejae University (Ergon learning suite).

## 뇌과학 아카데미 특강 (Oct 26 – Nov 6, 2026)

`index.html` is the showroom for Prof. Kwahk's Data Science & AI lectures in the Brain Science Academy for high-school students.

- **Showroom** → two booths (Week 1, Week 2) → pieces (lecture videos and Google Slides decks)
- Videos play in place through Google Drive's player; decks open in the Google Slides viewer
- The Drive files must be shared as "Anyone with the link · Viewer" for visitors to see them

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host. Pages: `#showroom`, `#week1`, `#week2`, `#w1v1`, `#w1v2`, `#w1v3`, `#w1s`, `#w2s`.
